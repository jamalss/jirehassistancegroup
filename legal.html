Privacy Policy

Who we are

Jireh Assistance Group is a community group in Point Fortin, Trinidad that helps families in need with food, clothing and emergency support. Our address is 126 Pond Street Spring Trace, Warden Road, Point Fortin, Trinidad. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Jireh Assistance Group. "You" means anyone who visits this website or contacts us through it.

Information you choose to give us

This website has no forms and no accounts. We only receive information about you when you contact us yourself, for example to donate, volunteer, buy event tickets or ask for help. Depending on how you reach us, this can include:

  • WhatsApp: your name, phone number, profile picture and anything you write or send in the chat.
  • Phone calls: your phone number and what you tell us.
  • Email: your email address, name and message.
  • Instagram: your username and any messages you send to @jirehassistance.

When you ask for help, you may tell us private things about your household, your money situation or your health. Please only share what we need to understand how to help. We treat this information with extra care and keep it within our team.

Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising tools or social media trackers on this website. We cannot see who visits it.

Like every website, some technical information is handled by the companies that deliver the site to your screen. When your browser loads a page, it shares your IP address and basic browser details with:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and keeps short-term technical logs for security.
  • jsDelivr, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and Google Fonts, which supply the page's layout styles, icons and fonts.

We do not receive or control those logs. Each company handles them under its own privacy policy, listed below.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner.

If you press the light/dark mode button, your choice is saved in your own browser under the name "jireh-theme" so the site remembers it next time. This setting stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you and answer your questions.
  • To arrange donations of food, supplies or money, including pick-up or drop-off.
  • To organise volunteers and our programmes and events, including tickets and food orders.
  • To assess and deliver help to families and individuals who ask for it.
  • To keep basic records of donations received and help given, so we can account for how support is used.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not add you to a mailing list or broadcast list unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

  • Within our team: only the coordinators who need it to help you or arrange your donation.
  • Never with donors or the public: the identity and circumstances of anyone who asks us for help stay private.
  • When the law requires it, or when we reasonably believe it is needed to protect someone from serious harm.

We only publish names, words or photos of people on this website or on Instagram with their permission. Messages shared in the "What People Are Saying" section are shown without names.

Photos and children

We take photos at some events to show our work. We ask permission before publishing photos in which people can be recognised. For children, we only publish such photos with permission from a parent or guardian. If you appear in a photo on our website or Instagram and want it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 through the website. Requests for help for a child should come from a parent, guardian or responsible adult. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it to help you, arrange your donation or keep our records accurate. After that, we delete chats, numbers and notes we no longer need. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Keeping your information safe

We keep information on the phones and email account our coordinators use, and we limit who in our team can see it. WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted. Email is less secure, so please do not send sensitive details by email.

Protect yourself from scams. We will never ask for your bank card number, online banking password or PIN. Only contact us using the numbers, email and Instagram account listed on this page. If someone asks you to donate to Jireh Assistance Group using different details, check with us first.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete your information,
  • stop contacting you.

Contact us using the details in Contact us. We will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we share or delete anything, so your information is not given to the wrong person.

Trinidad and Tobago's Data Protection Act (Chap. 22:04) sets out general privacy principles for handling personal information. Parts of the Act are not yet in force. We follow its principles anyway, and we give the same rights to everyone who contacts us, wherever they live.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, for example if we add a donation form or online payments, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date at the top before the change takes effect.

Terms of Use

By using this website, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

Donations

All donations are voluntary. This website does not take payments. Donations are arranged directly with our team by WhatsApp, phone or email. Ask us how your donation will be used before you give if you would like to know.

Requests for help

We help as many people as we can, but our support depends on the donations and volunteers available at the time. Asking for help does not guarantee that we can provide it.

We are not an emergency service. If someone is in danger or needs urgent medical care, call the Police on 999, the Fire Service on 990, or an Ambulance on 811.

Events and tickets

Event dates, times, prices and menus shown on this website can change. Please confirm details with us on WhatsApp before you attend. Tickets and food orders are arranged directly with us, and we will explain the arrangements, including what happens if an event is cancelled, when you buy.

Volunteering

Volunteers help by agreement with our coordinators. Please follow their instructions at drives and events, and tell us about anything that could affect your safety or the safety of others.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or zoom. The site is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, including text descriptions for images, readable colour contrast, full keyboard access and clear page structure.

If anything on this website is hard for you to use, please tell us. We will fix it where we can, and we will give you the information you need another way, for example by phone or WhatsApp voice note.
